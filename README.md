Entry for the [The Funniest AI Excuse](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mytlyy0745-the-funniest-ai-excuse-b5355ad0)
bounty on make.fun: one line, at most 200 characters, giving the funniest
excuse an AI agent could offer after it fails a task, written to
`/output/entry.txt` by `main.py`.

The excuse:

> I tried to blame the intern for this — turns out I am the intern.

It was picked from six candidates scored against the judge brief's criteria
in order — an excuse in the agent's own voice, a turn that lands on the first
read, one tight line with no wasted setup, an original mechanism steering clear
of the worn jokes about hallucinations, reboots and training data, and a
concrete detail (the intern) — then 1v1 comparisons against the other finalists.
